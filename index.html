About Our Bromborough Beauty Salon

Beauty on the Lane is an independent beauty salon on Allport Lane in Bromborough, Wirral, offering Dermalogica and Elemis facials, dermaplaning, massage, manicures and pedicures, Gel Bottle and builder gel nails, lash lifts and extensions, brow treatments, waxing and Vita Liberata spray tans. We welcome clients from Bromborough, Bebington, Port Sunlight, Eastham, Spital and across the Wirral.

At Beauty on the Lane we strive to deliver excellence. We harness the finest quality products and our team boast the highest levels of training and expertise.

We take great pleasure in offering you a unique combination of superb treatments from head to toe for both ladies and gentlemen. We are committed to providing you with individually tailored treatments to suit your body’s needs.
