About Us

Beauty on the Lane is a beauty salon on Allport Lane in the heart of Bromborough village, Wirral. We strive to deliver excellence, harnessing the finest quality products — including Dermalogica and Elemis skincare — and our team boast the highest levels of training and expertise.

We take great pleasure in offering you a unique combination of superb treatments from head to toe for both ladies and gentlemen, from facials, massage and dermaplaning to nails, lashes, brows, waxing and spray tans. We are committed to providing you with individually tailored treatments to suit your body’s needs.

Whether you’re looking for a relaxing massage, a Dermalogica or Elemis facial, gel nails, a lash lift or a Vita Liberata spray tan, we’d love to look after you. Get in touch to book your appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Beauty on the Lane located?

You’ll find us at 8 Allport Lane in the heart of Bromborough village, Wirral, CH62 7HP. We welcome clients from across the Wirral, including Bebington, Eastham, New Ferry, Port Sunlight, Spital, Heswall, Birkenhead and Wallasey.

How do I book an appointment?

Call us on 0151 343 0877, message us on WhatsApp on 07742 014 037, or email beautyonthelanewirral@gmail.com and we’ll find a time that suits you.

Do I need a patch test before eye treatments?

Yes. A patch test is required at least 48 hours before tinting, lash lift and lash extension treatments to keep you safe.

Do you offer treatments for men?

Yes. We offer treatments from head to toe for both ladies and gentlemen, including massage, facials and waxing.
